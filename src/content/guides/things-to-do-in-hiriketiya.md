Hiriketiya is a place to slow down, but there's more to do than you'd think.

## In the bay

1. **Take a surf lesson.** The beach break is one of Sri Lanka's best places to learn. See [surf season](/guides/hiriketiya-surf-season/).
2. **Watch the surfers at sunset** from a café on the bay.
3. **Swim** in the calmer middle of the bay in season.
4. **Do yoga.** Several places run drop in classes, many on open air decks.
5. **Work from a café.** Hiriketiya is a remote work favourite ([long stays](/hiriketiya-long-term-rental/)).

## Nearby

6. **Wewurukannala Vihara (Dikwella):** a temple known for its giant seated Buddha, a few minutes inland from Dikwella.
7. **Hummanaya blowhole:** a natural blowhole a short tuk tuk ride east of Dikwella; most impressive when the swell is up.
8. **Dikwella beach:** a long, quiet stretch next door for a walk.
9. **Tangalle:** around 30 minutes east, with long wild beaches.
10. **Rekawa:** further east, known for sea turtle nesting (go with a responsible, licensed operator).

## Day trips

11. **Mirissa:** whale watching (in season, roughly November to April).
12. **Galle Fort:** the UNESCO listed old town, about 1h15 away.
13. **Mulkirigala:** a rock temple with cave shrines and views over the jungle.
14. **Udawalawe National Park:** elephant safaris, around two hours inland.
15. **Scooter the coast:** rent a scooter (with a valid licence) and beach hop.
