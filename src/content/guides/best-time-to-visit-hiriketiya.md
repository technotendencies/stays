**Short answer:** the best time to visit Hiriketiya is **December to March**, when the south coast is dry and sunny and the surf is at its most consistent. **November and April** are excellent shoulder months with lower prices.

## The two seasons

Sri Lanka has two monsoons that hit different coasts at different times. For Hiriketiya on the south coast:

| Season | Months | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High season** | December – March | Dry, sunny, calm mornings, clean surf, busy and pricier |
| Shoulder | November, April | Mostly good weather, some showers, better prices |
| **Low season** | May – September | Southwest monsoon: rain, wind, bigger messy swell, very quiet |
| Inter-monsoon | October | Often the wettest, quietest month |

> Temperatures barely change: expect roughly 27–31°C all year. The difference is rain, wind and sea conditions.

## Peak season tips

- Book the best stays early — Christmas to New Year and February sell out months ahead.
- Popular places can have minimum-night rules over the holidays.
- See [the best hotels in Hiriketiya](/best-hotels-in-hiriketiya/) to shortlist early.

## Low season tips

- Monthly rates drop a lot — ideal for [long stays](/hiriketiya-long-term-rental/).
- The east coast (Arugam Bay) is in season from roughly May to September if you want surf.
