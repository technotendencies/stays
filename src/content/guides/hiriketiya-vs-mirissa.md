Both are south coast favourites less than an hour apart, but they feel very different.

## Quick comparison

| | Hiriketiya | Mirissa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size** | One small bay | Long beach + a busy town strip |
| **Vibe** | Laid back, surf & remote work | Lively, more nightlife |
| **Surf** | Great for beginners + a reef | Some breaks, not the main draw |
| **Swimming** | Good in the calm middle (in season) | Good in sheltered sections |
| **Accommodation** | Guesthouses, boutique hotels, villas | Everything, incl. larger hotels |
| **Famous for** | Surf, cafés, the bay | Whale watching, Coconut Tree Hill, parties |

## Choose Hiriketiya if…

- You want to **learn to surf** or surf every day
- You prefer **small and walkable** over busy and big
- You're staying **longer** or working remotely

## Choose Mirissa if…

- You want **nightlife** and lots of bars and restaurants
- **Whale watching** is a priority
- You want a **big hotel** with full services

## Why not both?

They're under an hour apart. Base yourself in Hiriketiya and do Mirissa as a day trip for whale watching.
