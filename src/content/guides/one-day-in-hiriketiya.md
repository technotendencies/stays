One day is not enough for Hiriketiya, but it is enough to fall for it. This is how we would spend it.

## Morning: breakfast and the bay

Start at [Malkoha](/local/malkoha/), our top pick for breakfast and the best coffee in town. Then head down to the bay: watch the surfers, swim in the calm middle of the bay in season, or book a lesson on the beach break.

## Afternoon: a long lunch with a view

Walk over to [The Ruin](/local/the-ruin/), one of our favourite places to spend the whole afternoon: good food, good drinks and a view you will not want to leave. If you want something cheaper, [Roti Hut](/local/roti-hut/) is simple, local and genuinely good.

Getting stiff from the surf? [Massage Shed](/local/massage-shed/) is our favourite place for a massage.

## Sunset

Go to [Pehebiya Beach](/local/pehebiya-beach/), right next door and one of the local favourites for sunset, or grab a drink at [Raa](/local/raa/) on the beach. Get there half an hour before the sun goes down.

## Night

Dinner at [Smoke & Bitters](/local/smoke-and-bitters/) for cocktails and a proper meal. After that, see [tonight in Hiriketiya](/tonight/) for parties, karaoke and the beer garden at [ClearPoint](/local/clearpoint-beer-garden/).
