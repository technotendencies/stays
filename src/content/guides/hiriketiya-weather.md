Hiriketiya has a tropical climate: hot and humid all year, with the seasons defined by **rain and wind**, not temperature.

## At a glance

- **Air temperature:** about 27 to 31°C year round
- **Sea temperature:** about 27 to 29°C, so no wetsuit needed
- **Dry season:** December to March
- **Wet season:** May to September (southwest monsoon), plus a wet October

## Dry season (December to March)

Blue skies, calm mornings, light offshore winds and the flattest seas for swimming. This is peak season for a reason. Book [popular stays](/best-hotels-in-hiriketiya/) early.

## Shoulder months (April, November)

Mostly sunny with more afternoon showers and humidity. Great value and still good surf.

## Monsoon (May to September)

The southwest monsoon brings rain, onshore wind and rougher seas to the south coast. It rarely rains all day. Expect heavy bursts and sunny spells. It's a lovely time for a quiet, green, cheap stay in a [villa with a pool](/hiriketiya-villas/).
