For a bay this small, Hiriketiya punches far above its weight on food. Almost everything is within a ten-minute walk of the beach.

## Breakfast & coffee

Expect smoothie bowls, sourdough, eggs every way and genuinely good coffee along the bay road. Go early in peak season — the post-surf rush starts around 9am.

## Sri Lankan food

Don't leave without a proper **rice & curry**: a mound of rice with five or six small curries, sambols and papadums. Local places a few minutes up from the beach serve it at lunch for a fraction of café prices. Also look out for **kottu** in the evening and **hoppers** (crispy bowl-shaped pancakes) at breakfast.

## Seafood & dinner

Fresh fish from the Dikwella boats — grilled whole, in a curry or as sashimi at the more upmarket spots. Wood-fired pizza has become a Hiriketiya staple too.

## Sunset drinks

Grab a spot facing the bay at golden hour. Things are relaxed rather than rowdy; the bay gets livelier at weekends in peak season.

## Tips

- Many places close or cut hours in the monsoon (May–September).
- Cash is useful for smaller local spots.
- Most of the best food is on or near the bay — [stay close](/hiriketiya-beachfront-hotels/) and you'll never need a tuk-tuk for dinner.
