Three days is the sweet spot: enough to surf, eat well, explore the coast and still have a lazy afternoon.

## Day 1: settle into the bay

- **Breakfast:** [Malkoha](/local/malkoha/) for breakfast and coffee.
- **Morning:** swim or take a surf lesson on the beach break.
- **Afternoon:** a long, slow afternoon at [The Ruin](/local/the-ruin/).
- **Sunset:** [Pehebiya Beach](/local/pehebiya-beach/), right next door.
- **Night:** see [what is on tonight](/tonight/).

## Day 2: explore by scooter

- **Breakfast:** hoppers at [Duni's Hopper](/local/dunis-hopper/), the local favourite.
- **Morning:** ride to [Blue Beach Island](/local/blue-beach-island/) in Nilwella, about 10 to 15 minutes away, for a swim and a snorkel.
- **Afternoon:** snorkel at [Turtle Point](/local/turtle-point/) near Bathigama. Keep your distance from the turtles and never touch them.
- **Evening:** sunset drinks at [Raa](/local/raa/) or the view from [Betaz](/local/betaz/).
- **Dinner:** the cheap and very good buffet at [Kave Waves](/local/kave-waves/).

## Day 3: slow down

- **Morning:** sauna and cold plunge at [Maha Sauna](/local/maha-sauna/), then a massage at [Massage Shed](/local/massage-shed/).
- **Lunch:** [La Playa](/local/la-playa/), an all day beach hangout.
- **If it is Saturday:** the local [Dikwella Market](/local/dikwella-market/).
- **Night:** dinner and cocktails at [Smoke & Bitters](/local/smoke-and-bitters/), then the beer garden at [ClearPoint](/local/clearpoint-beer-garden/) if you want to meet people.

> Hiriketiya changes fast. Event nights and opening hours move around, so check [tonight in Hiriketiya](/tonight/) and ask locally.
