Hiriketiya (you'll also see it spelled *Hiriketiya Bay* or just "Hiri") is a tiny, palm fringed horseshoe bay on Sri Lanka's south coast. Ten years ago it was a quiet fishing cove; today it's one of the island's favourite places to surf, work remotely and slow down, while still feeling small.

## Where is Hiriketiya?

Hiriketiya sits just east of **Dikwella** in the Matara District of the Southern Province. It's roughly:

- **3 hours** by car from Colombo or the airport (via the Southern Expressway)
- **1 hour 15 minutes** from Galle
- **40 to 50 minutes** from Mirissa
- **30 minutes** from Tangalle

## What is Hiriketiya like?

The whole bay is maybe 500 metres from end to end. A road loops around it, lined with cafés, surf schools and guesthouses. Behind that, the land climbs into coconut groves and jungle where most of the [villas](/hiriketiya-villas/) are hidden.

- **Surf:** a friendly beach break in the middle of the bay and a faster reef wave on the side.
- **Vibe:** barefoot, social, a mix of surfers, remote workers and couples.
- **Food:** far better than you'd expect for a place this size. See [Hiriketiya restaurants](/guides/hiriketiya-restaurants/).

## When to go

The main season on the south coast runs from **November to April**, with the driest, sunniest weather and most consistent surf from **December to March**. May to September is monsoon season: quieter, cheaper and wetter. Full breakdown in [the best time to visit Hiriketiya](/guides/best-time-to-visit-hiriketiya/).

## Where to stay

There are three simple options:

1. **On the bay:** walk to everything. Best for surfers and first timers. [Beachfront stays →](/hiriketiya-beachfront-hotels/)
2. **On the hill:** quieter, greener, most of the pool villas. [Villas →](/hiriketiya-villas/)
3. **Dikwella beach:** long, calm sand five minutes west, with bigger properties.

## How many days?

Three to four nights is a good minimum. Surfers and remote workers often stay weeks, and there are [places set up for long stays](/hiriketiya-long-term-rental/), with coworking and kitchens.
