Hiriketiya is on the south coast, just past Dikwella. Getting there is easy — the Southern Expressway does most of the work.

## Approximate travel times

| From | Private car | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colombo airport (CMB) | ~3–3.5 h | Via the Southern Expressway |
| Colombo city | ~3 h | |
| Galle | ~1 h 15 | Coastal road or expressway |
| Mirissa / Weligama | ~40–60 min | Coastal road |
| Tangalle | ~30 min | |
| Ella | ~3.5–4 h | Via the hill country |

> Times are estimates and vary with traffic, especially leaving Colombo.

## By private car or taxi (easiest)

Most visitors book a private driver or use a ride-hailing app. It's comfortable, door-to-door and — shared between two or more people — good value. Ask your stay: many can arrange pickups.

## By train

The scenic coastal railway runs from Colombo along the south coast to **Matara**, with an extension towards **Beliatta**. From there, take a tuk-tuk to Hiriketiya. Book reserved seats in advance in peak season.

## By bus

Express buses run along the Southern Expressway to Matara and beyond; local buses stop in **Dikwella**, a short walk or tuk-tuk from the bay. Cheapest option, least comfortable with luggage or surfboards.

## Getting around

Everything in the bay is walkable. For Dikwella, the hill villas or day trips, use tuk-tuks or rent a scooter (you'll need a valid licence and local permit).
